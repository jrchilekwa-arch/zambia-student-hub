💡 STUDY SMARTER

Study Tips

Simple habits that can help you study more effectively.

1

Create a Study Schedule

Set aside specific times for each subject and try to follow your schedule consistently.

2

Practise With Questions

Testing yourself helps you discover what you understand and what needs more revision.

3

Take Short Breaks

Regular short breaks can help you stay focused during longer study sessions.

4

Teach Someone Else

Explaining a topic in your own words is a powerful way to check your understanding.

5

Sleep Well

Give yourself enough time to rest so you can concentrate better when studying.

6

Start Early

Avoid leaving everything until the last minute. Small daily revision sessions add up.
