🧠 Study Smarter

Study Tips

Simple techniques to help you study more effectively.

⏰

Create a Study Schedule

Set specific times for studying and try to follow your schedule consistently.

📱

Reduce Distractions

Put unnecessary notifications and distractions away while you study.

✍️

Take Your Own Notes

Writing ideas in your own words can help you understand and remember them.

🔁

Review Regularly

Short regular revision sessions can be more effective than trying to learn everything at once.

📝

Practise Questions

Test yourself with questions instead of only reading your notes.

😴

Get Enough Rest

Good sleep and breaks can help you stay focused and learn effectively.

TRY THIS

The 25–5 Method

Study for 25 minutes, take a 5-minute break, then repeat.
